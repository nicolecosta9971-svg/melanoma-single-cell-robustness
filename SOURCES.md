# Sources and attribution

The original investigators recruited participants, collected biopsies, performed laboratory assays and sequencing, and supplied source clinical information and cell annotations. This project is a secondary computational reanalysis, not new biological data generation.

- Poźniak J et al. **A TCF4-dependent gene regulatory network confers resistance to immunotherapy in melanoma.** Cell, 2024. [DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2023.11.037](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.11.037).
- Poźniak J et al. **Cytotoxic NK Cells Impede Response to Checkpoint Immunotherapy in Melanoma with an Immune-Excluded Phenotype.** Cancer Discovery, 2025. [DOI 10.1158/2159-8290.CD-24-1208](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-24-1208).
- **KU Leuven Research Data Repository, DOI 10.48804/GSAXBN, version 7.** [Version-specific deposit](https://rdr.kuleuven.be/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.48804/GSAXBN&version=7.0).

The two papers concern the same cohort, not independent replications. Original investigators did not endorse or validate this reanalysis's endpoint merely because their studies are cited.

The selected research figures summarise real retained analyses. Both runnable Python examples are separately labelled as invented; they do not use anonymised or transformed research records. Source expression, clinical tables, article imagery, upstream code, trained models and atlases are not bundled.
