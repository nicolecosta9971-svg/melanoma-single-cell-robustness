# Figure captions

All four figures present stored **aggregate** summaries; preparing this package introduces no model fit or statistical test. They show no labelled individual patient trajectories/dots and do not establish anonymity, independent human review or clinical validation. Source attribution is in [references](REFERENCES.md); aggregate reuse and linked-information disclosure decisions remain open in [data availability](DATA_AVAILABILITY.md). Two copied figure footers were adapted to point to these captions rather than the complete non-bundled register; scientific values and graphical geometry are unchanged. The complete retained register preserves all settings, including refused and inconclusive analyses, outside this compact summary. These selected figures are not a count of independent confirmations.

## Figure 1. Measurement and participant accounting

[Participant flow](../figures/participant_flow.svg). Descriptive accounting within the released Poźniak immune-cell subset, not original recruitment flow. The processed input contains 25,178 immune cells and 22,897 RNA features; QC retains 24,361 cells across 45 baseline/on-treatment samples used for integration and annotation. The baseline endpoint uses 14,801 cells from 23 sample records representing 22 patients (10 R/12 NR). Sample log-ratios are averaged within patient. Additional cells or sample records do not create independent patient outcomes. Effect, uncertainty and p-value are not applicable to this accounting diagram. Source-to-export cell lineage and full recruitment/exclusions remain unresolved.

## Figure 2. Association alongside clinical, recovery and external uncertainty

[Association summary](../figures/association_summary.svg). Selected patient-level aggregate estimates; every displayed effect, 95% interval, group N and p-value is printed in the figure. Panel A shows the retrospective controlled Poźniak primary (10 R/12 NR: median difference 4.5988 log₂ units, interval 1.0392–6.5159, p=0.0134105) and its same-cohort best-overall-response-only sensitivity (6 R/11 NR). Panel B keeps GSE120575 (9 R/10 NR) and Schlenker (12 R+TF/10 PD) separate, with non-equivalent labels, denominators, platforms and response definitions. The fresh GSE120575 mapped result is not replaced by the older favourable preparation. Panel C shows post hoc yield-adjusted and secondary clinical **mean** effects in 10 R/12 NR Poźniak patients, not primary median estimands. In the joint sex/regimen model, the effect is 3.2542, HC3 interval −0.2171–6.7255, nominal p=0.0661514 and Holm-seven p=0.187682. Sparse clinical overlap and wide intervals limit interpretation. Median/AUC intervals use patient bootstrap; adjusted means use HC3 normal approximations. Same-cohort AUC is descriptive rank separation, not predictive accuracy. Means and medians cannot be directly subtracted to quantify attenuation; cohorts are not pooled. This is a selected display, not a complete evidence tally.

## Figure 3. All five predeclared processing fits

[Processing stability](../figures/processing_stability.svg). Same-cohort sensitivity analyses, each based on the same 10 R/12 NR baseline Poźniak patients. Every fit's median effect, 95% patient-bootstrap interval, nominal two-sided Mann–Whitney p-value and AUC/interval is displayed. All five seeds 0–4 are retained without choosing a preferred seed. The finite-setting ranges are median difference 4.3018–4.5677, AUC 0.8167–0.8500 and p=0.006211–0.013411, not population confidence intervals or independent replications. Majority-label agreement across all ten seed pairs is 0.860515–0.957555 on 24,361 baseline/on-treatment cells. The maximum absolute patient-score shift relative to the controlled vector is 3.4576 log₂ units, not the maximum pairwise-seed difference. Directional group stability does not establish accurate annotation, stable individual thresholds or exact historical reproduction.

## Figure 4. All nine patient-balanced annotation overlaps

[Annotation overlaps](../figures/annotation_overlap.svg). Same-data measurement sensitivity in 22 baseline Poźniak patients, 23 samples and 14,801 cells. All nine estimates and pointwise 95% whole-patient-bootstrap intervals are shown: Jaccard and both directional captures for narrow author cytotoxic CD8, author macrophage union and a contextual four-CD8-state union versus the fixed CellTypist populations. Ratios use joint sample fractions averaged within patient, then equally weighted patient masses; they are not pooled counts or means of patient-specific ratios. Bootstrap: 5,000 whole-patient resamples, seed 20261016, not response-stratified. No hypothesis/response test was performed; p-values are not applicable. Only 4.94% (3.06–7.02%) of fixed Tem/Trm mass overlaps the narrow author cytotoxic-CD8 state, compared with 80.71% (76.70–84.51%) for the contextual four-state union. These different definitions do not establish classification accuracy or biological ground truth. Cycling/doublet-labelled records remain in denominators; no labels or primary endpoint were changed.
