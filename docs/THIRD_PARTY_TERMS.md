## Permissions are conditional, not absent

[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) permits reuse subject to applicable attribution, noncommercial and share-alike conditions. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits reuse, including commercial reuse, under its conditions. Extra author permission is not automatically required for uses already permitted. Neither licence is a general privacy waiver.

Numerical facts, original prose, protected expression and database rights are not interchangeable; this note makes no blanket legal classification. Free hosting does not alone settle NonCommercial interpretation, and a CV-related use is not automatically commercial. Intended use and appropriate terms for scientific content remain decisions separate from the code licence. See the [official CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/).
